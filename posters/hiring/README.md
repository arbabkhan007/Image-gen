Design v2 — light editorial direction: full-bleed staff photo, cream notice
panel, emerald contact strip. Generated by `posters/hiring_v3.py` from
`posters/plate_wide.png`.

Both are exactly the ratios Meta recommends for single-image feed ads. JPGs are
quality 95 with 4:4:4 subsampling so the fine type stays sharp.

## Clearance built into the design

- **Top-left logo badge (≈176 × 56):** a light cream plate sitting on the dark
  wall of the photo — high contrast, nothing behind it to fight with. Drop the
  logo in and delete the "SCHOOL LOGO" guide text.
- **Emerald footer strip:** phone and email placeholders on the right, "Apply
  now" call to action on the left.
- Meta removed the `<20%` text rule from its auction in 2021, so the
  headline-led design is safe.

## Replace these placeholders before publishing

| Placeholder | Where | Replace with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SCHOOL LOGO` | top-right box | your logo, then remove the guide text |
| `0300 000 0000` | bottom-right, top line | your WhatsApp / admissions number |
| `careers@yourschool.edu.pk` | bottom-right, second line | your hiring email |

Edit the constants at the top of `posters/hiring_v3.py` (`ROLES`, `PHONE`,
`EMAIL`, `CTA`) and re-run:

```bash
python posters/hiring_v3.py
```

## Suggested Meta ad setup

- **Objective:** Leads (Instant Form) or Messages (WhatsApp).
- **Primary text:** "We're hiring! Montessori & Primary (English) teachers —
  female only — plus Physics and Urdu subject specialists for Matric. Send your
  CV today."
- **Headline:** Teachers Required — Apply Now
- **Audience:** Lahore + 25 km, ages 22–45, women for the Montessori/Primary
  roles (Meta allows gender targeting for jobs where a gender is a genuine
  occupational requirement; confirm with your local policy), interests:
  teaching, education, Montessori.
- **Both creatives** can be run in one ad set as separate ads to let Meta pick
  the better ratio.

Note: "Female only" for the Montessori/Primary roles reflects the school's
requirement as briefed. It is stated plainly on the creative for clarity.
